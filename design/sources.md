# Sources — The World in a Day, Sunday 22 November 2026

Six stories for the front page. Each was cross-checked in at least two
independent sources reporting on Sunday 22 November 2026 (morning roundups
and same-day wires). Single-source items were rejected.

## 1. LEAD — Russian cargo ship Sparta II burns in the Gulf of Finland

A sanctioned Russian cargo ship linked to the Defence Ministry, the
Sparta II, was hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland, caught fire and
drifted into Finnish waters off Loviisa. Its 16 crew evacuated by
lifeboat to a nearby Russian ship. Finland said Ukraine was likely
behind the attack; Finland's prime minister said the war is "spilling
over". The ship was bound from St Petersburg for Kaliningrad.

- CNN — "Finland warns Ukraine war 'spilling over' after Baltic Sea
  drone strike on Russian cargo ship" (22 Nov)
- The Guardian Ukraine war briefing, via europesays.com (22 Nov)
- Reuters reporting via staycurrentnews.com and tbsnews.net (22 Nov)
- New York Post (22 Nov)

## 2. Two tankers struck within an hour in the Strait of Hormuz

Two Hong Kong-flagged suezmax oil tankers (Torkel and Arina, per
Vanguard Tech) were hit by unidentified projectiles within about an
hour of each other near Oman's Khasab while leaving the Strait of
Hormuz; one crew was evacuated, no casualties were reported (UKMTO).
Iran's army said a tanker caught fire after striking a naval mine east
of the strait. Headline story across Sunday's world roundups.

- Reuters, via gCaptain — "Two Tanker Incidents Reported Near Oman"
- AFP, via europesays.com — "UK maritime agency: 2 tankers attacked in
  Strait of Hormuz"
- TradeWinds — "Iran strikes two tankers in an hour while transiting
  Strait of Hormuz"
- "World in 90 Seconds" Sunday briefing (22 Nov)

## 4. Iraq — fire at Hartha power station near Basra kills 4, injures 35

A fire at the Hartha thermal power station in northern Basra
governorate killed at least four people and injured 35, according to
Iraqi state media; the cause is unknown.

- Reuters, via the VIRAL SA METRO and Top-5-News roundups (22 Nov)

## 5. COP31 — draft deal drops fossil fuels, walkout threats

The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, ran into a second
overtime day after a draft deal removed all mentions of coal, oil and
gas, sparking threats of a walkout; the EU said it wants a good deal
or no deal. A genuinely new 22 Nov development of the COP31 story
(Saturday's edition covered the first overtime deadlock).

- RTM World — "World Today Top Stories" (22 Nov)
- The Daily Brief daily briefing (22 Nov)
- Desh Duniya Update news slides (22 Nov)

## 6. Cricket — India post 720/9, their highest Test total in New Zealand

India declared at 720 for 9 in Wellington — their highest Test total
in New Zealand, beating 520 at Hamilton in 2009 — built on centuries
from KL Rahul (136) and Rishabh Pant, taking control of the first
Test on day four, Sunday 22 Nov.
